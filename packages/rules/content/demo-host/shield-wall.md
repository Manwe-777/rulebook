## Description

The host digs in around the objectives and dares the enemy to come and take them.

## Detachment rule

### Interlocking Shields

While a DEMO HOST INFANTRY unit from your army is within range of an objective you control, each time an attack targets
it, worsen that attack's Armour Penetration by 1.

## Enhancements

### Banner of Resolve

The bearer's unit can re-roll Battle-shock tests.

### Old Campaigner

Once per battle, at the start of any phase, the bearer gains 1 Resolve point.

### Reinforced Plating

Upgrade. Each time an attack with a Strength higher than this unit's Toughness targets it, subtract 1 from the wound
roll.

## Stratagems

### Brace!

**When:** Your opponent's Shooting phase, just after an enemy unit selects its targets.

**Target:** One DEMO HOST INFANTRY unit from your army that was selected as a target.

**Effect:** Until the end of the phase, models in your unit have a 5+ invulnerable save.

### Fall Back in Order

**When:** Your Movement phase, just after a DEMO HOST unit from your army falls back.

**Target:** That unit.

**Effect:** Until the end of the turn, your unit can still shoot.
