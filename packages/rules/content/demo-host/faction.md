## Description

A made-up faction that shows every part of the format: characters that lead and support units, a transport, unit
upgrades, enhancements and detachments that can't be taken together.

## Army rule

### Hold the Line

At the start of your Command phase, gain 1 Resolve point for each objective you control. Each time a unit with this
ability is selected to shoot or fight, you can spend 1 Resolve point to re-roll its hit rolls of 1 until the end of the
phase.
